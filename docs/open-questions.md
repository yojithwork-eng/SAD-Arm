# Things we still need to figure out

- What voltage/current are the yellow gear motors actually rated for?
- Will the L298N handle both motors without overheating?
- Can our LM2596 boards keep up when the arm is doing real work?
- Does the 5V servo supply stay stable as the battery drains?
- What fuse size do we need for the finished wiring? Is it fitted yet?
- Are the lever connectors, wire sizes and servo plugs rated for the current?
- How do we power the Uno from buck #5 without feeding power back through USB?
- Is our 2S charger actually balance-charging, and how are we stopping over-discharge?
- Do the treads, motor shafts and arm brackets fit together?
- Do we stick with the PWM connections that work, or move to i-BUS later?
- What happens if the remote loses connection or someone needs to stop the robot quickly?

We'll answer these after measuring/testing, not by guessing.
